Power Law Lab

Venture investors expect most startups to fail and a few to win big. This simulator runs 3,000 imaginary funds so you can see what that does to the odds.

Assumptions

Every dollar goes into equal checks. Each startup lands in one outcome: lose everything (0x), break even (0.3x to 1.5x), good (2x to 6x), great (6x to 25x), or a home run (30x to 200x). You set the loss and home run odds, and the rest is split among the middle outcomes in a fixed ratio. With the default odds, the average startup returns about 1.4x.

These numbers are illustrative and are not taken from real fund data. Results ignore fees, time, and follow-on investing. Change the odds in model.js to test your own beliefs.
